About

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at Aarhus University. I am working at the Center for Humanities Computing (CHC) under the supervision of Professor Kristoffer Nielbo. I am part of the team building Lex AI service for the Danish national encyclopedia. My work covers the full retrieval-augmented pipeline, from retrieval and generation to evaluation and production deployment.

I also study whether LLMs behave like humans in well-known social psychology experiments. The details are given under the Publications.

I completed my PhD at the Universityof Helsinki under the supervision of Professor Jukka Nurminen . During my PhD, I worked on modeling the syntax of command-line commands, using methods that ranged from rule-based systems to fine-tuned language models to learn their structure.
